# Original upstream files

The links below point to the corresponding source paths in the upstream repository, [nathom/streamrip](https://github.com/nathom/streamrip), on its `dev` branch. They are useful for comparing the installed files shown in the Dolphin screenshot with upstream source.

**Important:** these links are upstream reference files, not backups of the files currently installed on your computer. The local files may differ because of your installed Streamrip version and your custom changes. GitHub's `dev` branch can also change over time.
